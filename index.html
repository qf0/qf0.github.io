Who.ru

I'm Mason Corkern from Denham Springs, Louisiana. Growing up, I became interested in technology when I discovered game modding on my Xbox 360. Being homeschooled allowed me a lot of time to devote to learn cybersecurity and other topics. After I turned 18, I worked for Electronic Arts on Apex legends and then was hired by IBM as a programming apprentice. When I was 20, I moved to Chicago and worked for a cybersecurity consulting firm. Education wise I'm mostly an autodidact, but I've studied at Tulane and Berea College.

About This Weblog

This blog is where I post my ramblings, thoughts, write ups, and stories. I mainly post about things related to computer security. Aaron Swartz's weblog was a huge inspiration to start this project. I hope that you enjoy your stay.
